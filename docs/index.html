Key terms

Hospitalised injury
Admitted to hospital after a road crash and discharged alive.
Bed days
Total days those people spent in hospital.
Older group
People aged 65 and over.
n.p.
Not published: too small to release. Shown striped.
Left out
Records with no age, sex, road user or area (about 3%).
